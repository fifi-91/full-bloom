Beyond 1:1 coaching

Support designed around the people in the room.

My work has always centred on people at moments of change. Over more than 25 years, I’ve supported people across careers guidance, education, employability and personal development — from schools and higher education to community programmes and rehabilitation services.

Full Bloom brings that experience into flexible partnerships for organisations that want to give their people practical, human support. We can begin with an existing format or shape something around your audience, objectives and context.

Ways to work together

A flexible offer, built around what your people need.

01

Workshops & facilitated sessions

Interactive sessions around confidence, career direction, transferable skills, personal development, reflection and navigating change — designed for groups rather than delivered as a one-size-fits-all presentation.

02

Talks, panels & guest sessions

Thoughtful contributions for community events, professional networks, education settings and internal programmes where lived experience, practical guidance and an engaging coaching perspective can add value.

03

Career development support

Structured support for people navigating career choices, transitions, redundancy, return-to-work moments or a broader rethink of what comes next — informed by decades of careers guidance experience.

04

Community & women’s programmes

Partnered sessions and development experiences for community groups and women’s networks, with space for reflection, confidence-building, connection and practical next steps.

05

Bespoke organisational partnerships

If the need doesn’t fit neatly into one format, we can design the right one together — from a single session to a short series, a development programme or a tailored coaching collaboration.

How it works

Start with the need. Then shape the right response.

01

Tell me what you’re trying to achieve.

Share the audience, context, challenge and outcome you have in mind. It doesn’t need to be fully formed.

02

We shape the right format.

I’ll suggest an approach that fits the people involved — whether that is a workshop, talk, series or something more tailored.

03

We create something useful.

The goal is always the same: an experience that feels relevant in the room and leaves people with something they can actually use afterwards.
